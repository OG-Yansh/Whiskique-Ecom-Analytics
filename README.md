# Whiskique-Ecom-Analytics

## 🐱 Whiskique: Ecommerce Analytics Dashboard

An Interactive PowerBI dashboard to analyze the key Ecommerse KPIs of Whiskique, a fictatious pet supplies company, to answer questions about how to increase sales and reduce shipping costs by performing Market Basket and Shipping cost "What-if" analyses.

---

## 📄 Description

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed for *Whiskique*, a pet e-commerce business, focused on analyzing sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and operational efficiency. The dashboard consolidates key KPIs such as total sales, profit, profit margins, and shipping costs, while enabling deeper insights through market basket analysis, product-level profitability, and geographic sales distribution. It also incorporates a what-if simulation to evaluate the impact of order quantities on shipping costs, supporting data-driven decision-making for pricing, bundling, and cost optimization strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation
* 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data
* 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic

---

## 📂 Data Source

**Source:** DataCamp.com

Data on ~5,000+ e-commerce transactions from a pet products retailer, including order-level details on customers, products, quantities, sales, profit, and shipping costs, supported by dimension tables for ~100+ products, customer locations across multiple states/regions, and a complementary what-if dataset for simulating shipping cost changes based on quantity.

---

## 📌 Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

The business is focused on scaling revenue while controlling operational costs, particularly shipping. However, there is limited visibility into cross-sell opportunities, product mix optimization, and cost drivers impacting profitability.

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver a decision-support tool that enables:

* Revenue growth through targeted cross-sell and product expansion
* Cost optimization via shipping and quantity analysis
* Clear visibility into sales, profitability, and customer purchase behavior

---

### • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

* **Executive Summary:** Snapshot of core KPIs — Sales (~$1.55M), Profit (~$427K), Margin (~27.5%), Shipping (~$385K)
* **Product Performance:** Revenue and profit comparison across SKUs to identify high-impact products
* **Category Profitability:** Margin analysis highlighting high-return segments
* **Market Basket Analysis:** Frequently bought-together products to uncover bundling opportunities
* **Shipping Analysis:** Cost vs quantity trends and what-if simulation for cost optimization

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

* **Margin Expansion:** Electronics delivers the highest margin (~44.28%) but has minimal SKU presence → clear opportunity to scale this category
* **Cross-Sell Optimization:** Strong affinity between *Taste of the Wild Dog Food* and *Memory Foam Pet Beds* → viable combo/bundling strategy to increase AOV
* **Cost Efficiency:** Higher order quantities reduce per-unit shipping cost; Disposables show strong volume behavior → ideal for quantity-based promotions (e.g., Buy 2, Get 15% Off)
* **Strategic Outcome:** Enables simultaneous revenue growth and cost control through targeted, data-backed interventions
